## Pack 1: The Office

### 1. The Entrance
- **Length / people / setting:** 8s · 1 · glass-walled brokerage office, white marble, gold signage (no real brand).
- **Shots**
  1. Low wide shot at floor level → stilettos step through sliding glass doors.
  2. Medium tracking shot → she walks the aisle, coffee in hand, hair moving.
  3. Close-up → slow sunglasses-off, half-smile to camera.
- **Line (optional):** "Morning. Did I miss anything?"
- **Mood / tags:** confident, glam, arrival · office, heels, entrance
- **Suggested cast:** a Luxury lifestyle or Business & finance preset (e.g. Amira Al Qasimi, Baja Savage).

### 2. Whisper at the Desk
- **Length / people / setting:** 10s · 2 · shared desk, ring lights, city view behind.
- **Shots**
  1. Over-the-shoulder → Agent A leans in to Agent B's laptop.
  2. Two-shot → whispering, B's eyes widen.
  3. Both look up together at someone off-camera; cut to black.
- **Line:** A: "Don't say anything. She's already here."
- **Mood / tags:** gossip, tension, comedic beat · office, whisper, reaction
- **Suggested cast:** two Beauty & skincare presets (e.g. Valeria Mendoza + Chloe Tan).

### 3. The Listing Pitch
- **Length / people / setting:** 12s · 2 · corner office, the boss behind a desk (boss can be any influencer).
- **Shots**
  1. Wide → agent places a glossy listing book on the desk.
  2. Close-up → she taps the price.
  3. Reverse → the boss leans back, unconvinced.
  4. Close-up → "Give me thirty days."
- **Line:** "Forty-two million. I already have two buyers."
- **Mood / tags:** ambitious, sharp · office, pitch, negotiation
- **Suggested cast:** Business & finance presets (e.g. Yara Al Harbi pitching, Tara Singhania as the boss).

## Pack 2: The Listing

### 4. The Reveal
- **Length / people / setting:** 10s · 2 · hillside modern villa, floor-to-ceiling pocket doors.
- **Shots**
  1. Medium → agent grips both door handles, looks back at the client.
  2. Wide → doors slide apart, light floods in, infinity pool and skyline.
  3. Client reaction close-up → speechless.
- **Line:** Agent: "And this is the backyard."
- **Mood / tags:** wow, aspirational · villa, pool, view, reveal
- **Suggested cast:** agent from Luxury lifestyle (e.g. Rhea Malhotra); client any preset.

### 5. Walk & Sell
- **Length / people / setting:** 12s · 2 · double-height marble great room, chandelier.
- **Shots**
  1. Steadicam walk → agent and client side by side.
  2. Insert → her hand glides over a waterfall marble island.
  3. Medium → she turns, points up at the chandelier.
  4. Close-up → client nods, smiling.
- **Line:** "Italian marble, twenty-foot ceilings, and the primary suite has its own terrace."
- **Mood / tags:** polished, persuasive · showing, walk-and-talk
- **Suggested cast:** Giulia Moretti or Sofia Bellini.

### 6. Broker's Open
- **Length / people / setting:** 10s · 3 · open-house living room, catering, champagne tower.
- **Shots**
  1. Wide → agents mingling, glasses clinking.
  2. Door → a rival agent walks in, everyone turns.
  3. Close-up → host's smile tightens.
- **Line:** Host (under her breath): "Who invited her?"
- **Mood / tags:** social, frosty · open house, champagne, rival
- **Suggested cast:** Samira Jaffer (host), Monica Castellanos (rival), plus one Luxury lifestyle guest.

### 7. Offer Accepted
- **Length / people / setting:** 8s · 1 · villa terrace at golden hour.
- **Shots**
  1. Medium → phone rings, she answers, paces.
  2. Close-up → stops, mouth opens.
  3. Wide → small jump, spins toward the view.
- **Line:** "Full asking? Cash? ... Send it over."
- **Mood / tags:** triumphant · phone call, sunset, win
- **Suggested cast:** Tatiana Morales or Bianca Reyes.

## Pack 4: The Drama

### 11. Patio Showdown
- **Length / people / setting:** 12s · 2 · poolside patio, loungers, evening.
- **Shots**
  1. Two-shot → they stand too close, arms crossed.
  2. Close-up A → "Who told you that?"
  3. Close-up B → a long pause, then a smirk.
  4. Wide → A turns away to the pool.
- **Line:** A: "Who told you that?" / B: "Does it matter if it's true?"
- **Mood / tags:** confrontational, cinematic · argument, pool, night
- **Suggested cast:** Maya Hassan vs Zoe Mansour.

### 12. The Walk-Off
- **Length / people / setting:** 8s · 1 · glass hallway to the street.
- **Shots**
  1. Medium → she grabs her bag mid-sentence.
  2. Tracking → sunglasses on, quick walk.
  3. Exterior → doors close behind her.
- **Line:** "I'm done here."
- **Mood / tags:** dramatic exit · walk-off, sunglasses, doors
- **Suggested cast:** Salma Benali or Astrid van der Berg.
